========================================================================
 AL-RIWAYAT — HOW TO CREATE AN ADVERTISEMENT
 A plain-language guide to every field in the Ad editor
========================================================================

Open:  Admin  ->  Ads (sidebar, under "Advertising")  ->  "New advertisement"

An ad is two images (one for desktop, one for mobile) that show in one or more
fixed slots on the site and link to one URL when tapped. It only goes live when
its status is "published" AND today is inside its start/end date window.

REQUIRED to save any ad (draft or published):
  - Title
  - At least one Placement
  - Desktop image AND Mobile image
  - Click-through URL
  - Start date AND End date

A single slot shows at most TWO ads at once, stacked one below the other. If
more than two ads are eligible for the same slot, the two with the highest
Priority win; the rest wait until one of those ends.

------------------------------------------------------------------------
 SECTION 1 — BASICS
------------------------------------------------------------------------

TITLE (internal)          [required]
    A name only YOU see, to find the ad later in the list.
    It is NEVER shown to readers.
    Example: "Ramadan Sale - Brand X - July"

CLIENT
    Which advertiser this ad belongs to. Pick from the list of clients you
    created in the Clients tab. Optional — you can leave it as "No client".
    Used for your own reporting (e.g. "show me all of Brand X's ads").
    The client's name is copied onto the ad, so deleting a client later
    does not blank out old ads.

PLACEMENTS                [required - pick one OR MORE]
    WHERE on the site the ad appears. Tick every slot you want it to run in:
      - Home - community section  (between the reels and the comments)
      - Weekly article - in-line  (in the middle of a weekly article)
      - Weekly article - after body  (right after a weekly article ends)
      - Issue page - before share  (near the bottom of an issue)
    You cannot invent new spots — these are the fixed slots wired into the
    site. One ad can run in several slots at once. A slot shows at most two
    ads (stacked); if more qualify, higher Priority wins.

STATUS
    - draft      = saved but NOT shown to readers (work in progress)
    - published  = eligible to show (still must pass the schedule window)
    - archived   = retired, kept for records, never shown
    Tip: leave as draft while setting up, publish when ready. You can also
    publish/unpublish quickly from the ad list without opening the editor.

------------------------------------------------------------------------
 SECTION 2 — CREATIVES (the images)
------------------------------------------------------------------------

Why two images?  A wide desktop banner looks squashed on a phone. So you can
upload one image shaped for desktop and (optionally) a second shaped for
phones. The site automatically shows the right one for each screen.

DESKTOP IMAGE             [required to publish]
    The main image. Shown on laptops/desktops, and on phones too IF you do
    not provide a mobile image.
    Recommended size is shown in the form and depends on the placement
    (roughly a wide banner, e.g. 1200 x 450).

MOBILE IMAGE              [required]
    A phone-friendly version — squarer than the desktop one (e.g. 800 x 800).
    Since most visitors are on phones, a proper mobile image is required:
    a wide desktop banner shown on a phone shrinks to an unreadable strip.

  HOW UPLOAD WORKS (important):
    - Click "Choose image", pick a file (JPEG, PNG, WebP, GIF, or AVIF).
    - Max size 4 MB per image.
    - You will see a preview immediately with a small "Uploads on save" tag.
    - Nothing is actually uploaded yet. The image is sent to storage only
      when you click "Save". So if you hit Cancel, nothing is left behind.
    - "Replace" swaps the image; the old one is discarded on save.

ALT TEXT
    A short text description of the image, read aloud by screen readers for
    blind/low-vision users (accessibility). Not visible on screen.
    Example: "Brand X storefront with Ramadan discount banner"

IMAGE SHAPE — no cropping:
    Whatever you upload is shown IN FULL. The image is never cropped or cut
    at the edges. It stretches to the slot's width, and if that would make it
    too tall, it shrinks (centred) to respect a maximum height instead.
    So any aspect ratio works — you do not have to match an exact size.
    Practical advice:
      - Desktop image: use a WIDE shape (banner-like, e.g. 1200 x 450).
      - Mobile image: use a SQUARER shape (e.g. 800 x 800). A very wide
        banner on a phone becomes tiny and unreadable.
    Text in the image should be large enough to read on a small screen.

Note: every ad automatically shows a small grey "Sponsored" label in the
top corner. You do not add this — it is added for you, to tell readers the
content is a paid promotion.

------------------------------------------------------------------------
 SECTION 3 — LINK & CHANNELS
------------------------------------------------------------------------

CLICK-THROUGH URL         [needed for the ad to be clickable]
    The ONE web address that opens when a reader taps the ad.
    Must start with http:// or https://.
    Example: https://brandx.com/ramadan
    If you leave this empty, the ad still shows but is not clickable.

OPEN IN NEW TAB   (checkbox)
    On  = tapping opens the link in a new browser tab (recommended, keeps
          the reader on Al-Riwayat).
    Off = opens in the same tab.

CHANNELS (internal tags)
    These four buttons are ONLY for your own records/filtering. They do NOT
    change what the reader sees or where the tap goes. Turn on any that
    apply — you can pick more than one:
      - Website
      - Instagram Reel
      - Instagram Status
      - WhatsApp
    When you turn a channel ON, an extra box appears so you can store that
    channel's own link/number for your reference:
      - Website          -> a website URL
      - Instagram Reel   -> the reel URL
      - Instagram Status -> the story/status URL
      - WhatsApp         -> a phone number + an optional prefilled message
    Reminder: the READER's tap always uses the "Click-through URL" above,
    not these. These are just notes/metadata for you and the client.

------------------------------------------------------------------------
 SECTION 4 — SCHEDULE & DELIVERY
------------------------------------------------------------------------

STARTS / ENDS  (dates)   [both required]
    The window during which a PUBLISHED ad is allowed to show. Outside this
    window the ad simply does not appear (and no empty gap is left on the
    page). The end date must be on or after the start date.

PRIORITY  (number, default 0)
    If several ads are eligible for the SAME slot at the same time, the one
    with the HIGHER priority wins and is shown. Higher number = wins.

WEIGHT  (number, default 1)
    A tie-breaker among ads of equal priority — think of it as "share of
    rotation". An ad with weight 3 is favoured over one with weight 1.
    (In this first version, ties are broken by weight; leave at 1 if unsure.)

MAX IMPRESSIONS  (optional)
    A cap on how many times the ad may be SEEN before it auto-stops.
    Leave empty for unlimited. Use this when a client paid for a fixed
    number of views.

MAX CLICKS  (optional)
    Same idea, but caps total clicks. Leave empty for unlimited.
    When either cap is reached, the ad stops serving automatically.

SHOW ON DEVICES
    Restrict the ad to "mobile" only, "desktop" only, or leave BOTH off to
    show on all devices (the normal case).

------------------------------------------------------------------------
 SECTION 5 — TRACKING (UTM)  [optional, for analytics]
------------------------------------------------------------------------

UTM tags get added to the click link so tools like Google Analytics on the
CLIENT's own website can see the traffic came from Al-Riwayat.

    SOURCE     e.g.  al-riwayat
    MEDIUM     e.g.  banner  or  sponsored
    CAMPAIGN   e.g.  ramadan-2026

CAMPAIGN ID
    Your own internal reference code to group ads that belong to one
    campaign. Free text.

Note: Al-Riwayat also tracks its OWN impressions and clicks for every ad
automatically (no setup needed) — see the "Stats" button on each ad in the
list. UTM is only for the client's external analytics.

------------------------------------------------------------------------
 SECTION 6 — NOTES
------------------------------------------------------------------------

NOTES
    Free-text internal notes about this ad (e.g. "client wants swap on the
    15th"). Only you see this.

========================================================================
 QUICK CHECKLIST TO PUBLISH A WORKING AD
========================================================================
  [ ] Title filled
  [ ] One or more Placements ticked
  [ ] Desktop image uploaded
  [ ] Mobile image uploaded
  [ ] Click-through URL filled (starts with http:// or https://)
  [ ] Start date and End date set
  [ ] Status set to "published"
  [ ] Click "Save"
  -> Open the matching page on the site to confirm it shows.
     (If nothing appears: check status is published, the date window covers
      today, and any impression/click cap is not already reached.)

========================================================================
 AFTER IT'S LIVE — SEEING RESULTS
========================================================================
  In the Ads list, click "Stats" on any ad to see:
    - Impressions (times it was actually seen on screen)
    - Clicks and CTR (click-through rate = clicks / impressions)
    - Unique devices (how many DIFFERENT visitors saw it, so you can tell
      reach apart from repeat views)
    - A day-by-day chart over 7 / 30 / 90 days
    - Mobile vs desktop split
    - How close it is to its impression/click cap
  Use these numbers to report performance back to the client.

  Privacy note: "unique devices" is counted from an anonymized fingerprint
  (a one-way hash of IP + browser). Raw IP addresses are NOT stored.
